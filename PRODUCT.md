# Legend Games — Durable Product Specification (`PRODUCT.md`)

## 1. Product Overview & Purpose
**Legend Games** (also branded as *NaijaPlay / Legend Gaming Hub*) is a premier gaming hardware, game installation, custom console wrapping, and repair service platform based in Nigeria.

The platform provides a streamlined **Quote & Request Builder** where gamers can configure consoles (PS3, PS4, PS5, Xbox), customize with physical/digital game packages, choose custom console wraps, purchase gaming accessories, configure hard disk game loading, or submit repair & trade-in requests in a single unified order.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Market
- **Audience**: Console and PC gamers, esports enthusiasts, and game center operators in Nigeria.
- **Currency**: Nigerian Naira (**₦**) exclusively.
- **Operating Context**: High mobile traffic, varied network speeds, strong reliance on direct WhatsApp communication for order finalization.

---

## 3. Core Business Model & Non-Negotiables

### A. Quote & Request System (Not Live Checkout)
- Customers build an itemized multi-category order (consoles + games + wraps + accessories + repair notes) and submit it as one request.
- The shop owner reviews the request and connects directly via WhatsApp to confirm real-time inventory, finalize pricing, and schedule a handover.
- **Safety First**: Delivery/handover occurs exclusively at verified, neutral, and safe public meetup locations (no blind home deliveries).

### B. Free Annual Football Game Bundle
- Every PS4 and PS5 purchase automatically includes the current year's FC edition (e.g., FC26 in 2026) at no extra charge.

### C. Game Installation Modes
- **Online-Added (PSN Safe)**: Official digital licenses that permit online multiplayer and PSN sign-in. Higher cost per game.
- **Offline-Modded (Jailbreak / Hen)**: Sideloaded / modded games at significantly reduced cost. Must explicitly display non-removable warning: *"Offline only — connecting to PSN will disable installed games"*.

### D. Hard Disk Game Loading (PS4 & PC)
- Supports selectable disk capacities (1TB, 2TB, 3TB+).
- Dynamic storage meter calculates real-time space usage per game (PS4 vs PC install sizes differ) with an automatic 10-15% filesystem buffer protection.

### E. Custom Console Wraps & Skins
- High-visual showcase offering custom wraps (e.g. Spider-Man, God of War, Cyberpunk) as an add-on or standalone service.

### F. Multi-Purpose Repair & Trade-In Portal
- Unified form handling Repairs (with issue description & photo uploads), Game Loading, and Console Trade-Ins (graded on condition: Like New / Good / Fair / For Parts).

---

## 4. Voice & Tone
- **Tone**: Enthusiastic, trustworthy, transparent, street-smart, and gaming-native.
- **Clarity**: Unambiguous itemization; upfront distinctions between modded vs unmodded options to avoid customer confusion.
