How buying works at Legend Games

We do not use automatic card debits or risky home deliveries. Here is our 4-step safety workflow.

1

Build Your Setup & Submit Request

Browse our consoles (PS5, PS4, Xbox, PS3), select your firmware (Normal vs Modded), add game discs, hard drive loadings, and accessories. Submit your basket to generate your order reference code.

2

WhatsApp Confirmation (in <5 mins)

Your order opens directly in WhatsApp. We verify current inventory, confirm total pricing with zero hidden charges, and provide photos/videos of your exact hardware upon request.

3

Agree on a Safe Public Meetup Location

We agree on a secure, public landmark in Lagos (e.g. Ikeja City Mall, Maryland Mall, Lekki Phase 1, Surulere). We NEVER do secluded or home deliveries for maximum buyer and seller security.

4

Inspect & Test, Then Pay

Meet up, inspect your console, games, and accessories thoroughly. Once you are 100% satisfied with the condition and testing, you make your payment (bank transfer or cash) on the spot!
