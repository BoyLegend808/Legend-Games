FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our consoles, firmware, and meetups

What is the difference between Normal and Modded / Hacked consoles? +

Normal (Original Firmware): Operates on official Sony/Microsoft firmware. You can play online on PSN/Xbox Live, stream apps, and play with friends online. Games are purchased via official digital stores or physical discs.

Modded / Hacked (Jailbroken): Operates on custom firmware (e.g. PS4 9.00 / 11.00 or PS3 CFW). You can install dozens of blockbuster games directly onto the hard drive for a fraction of the cost. However, you cannot play online on PSN. Best for gamers who want huge game libraries at high savings.

Why do you only do safe public meetups and no home delivery? +

Safety for both our customers and our team is our highest priority. Meeting at known, secure landmarks in Lagos (malls, shopping centers) allows you to inspect the console, verify serial numbers, test it, and pay on the spot with complete peace of mind.

How does the free EA Sports FC bundle work? +

Every PS4 and PS5 console purchased from Legend Games automatically includes a free copy of that calendar year's EA Sports FC title (currently EA Sports FC 26). It is bundled free into your purchase automatically!

What happens if a game I want isn't on the catalog? +

You can request any title using our "Request a game not listed" box or by chatting with us on WhatsApp. We have supplier connections across Lagos and can source virtually any PS3, PS4, PS5, Xbox or PC game for you.

How does hard disk game loading work? +

You pick a hard drive capacity (1TB, 2TB, 4TB), choose your favorite titles, and we pre-load the drive with direct-install game packages. When you plug it into your jailbroken PS4 or PC, the games are immediately accessible and ready to play without downloading.
